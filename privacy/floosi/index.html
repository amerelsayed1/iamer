Privacy Policy

Floosi: Budget & Debt Tracker · Last updated: November 15, 2026

This policy explains how the mobile app Floosi: Budget & Debt Tracker (the “App”), published by Amer Mahsoub (“I”, “me”), handles information. The App is a personal budget, expense and debt tracker. It has no accounts or sign-in, shows no ads, and keeps your financial records on your phone.

Information stored on your device

Everything you enter is stored locally on your device and is not received by me: accounts and balances, transactions, categories, budgets, debts, monthly commitments, assets, notes, and any receipt photos you attach. Uninstalling the App or clearing its storage removes this data.

Auto-capture from SMS and notifications (optional)

If you turn on Auto-capture in Settings and approve Android’s permission prompt, the App reads new incoming SMS messages (the RECEIVE_SMS permission) and, if you separately allow it, notifications from bank and wallet apps.

  • Each message is checked on your device. If it does not mention an amount of money, it is discarded immediately and never stored. Chat and email apps are skipped.
  • A message that mentions an amount becomes a draft transaction stored on your device. Nothing is added to your balances until you tap Add.
  • This data is used only to create those drafts and to suggest the account and category you chose before. It is never uploaded, sold or shared.
  • The App does not send, change or delete SMS, and does not read your old messages or your call log.

You can turn Auto-capture off at any time in the App’s Settings or in your device’s Android settings.

Camera and photos (optional)

If you attach a receipt, the App uses your camera or photo picker. The image is saved on your device with the transaction.

Backup and export (optional)

If you choose to back up, you sign in with your Google account and the App saves a backup file in the hidden app-data folder of your own Google Drive. Only the App can open that folder; I cannot see your Drive or your backup. You can delete it in the App or in your Google Drive settings. You can also export your transactions as a CSV file, which stays on your device unless you choose to share it.

Information collected automatically

The App uses Google Firebase services, which may collect:

  • App activity — how you use the App, collected through Firebase Analytics to understand and improve it.
  • Crash logs and diagnostics — collected through Firebase Crashlytics to find and fix crashes.
  • Performance data — such as app start time, collected through Firebase Performance Monitoring.
  • Device and app information — device model, operating system and app version, and an app-instance identifier.

I do not put your amounts, account names or message text in these reports. The advertising ID is not collected: it is turned off in Firebase Analytics. The App does not request location permission and does not collect your name, email address, phone number or contacts.

The App also fetches public exchange rates and gold prices from third-party services (such as open.er-api.com and goldprice.org). These requests contain none of your data, but, like any internet request, they reveal your IP address to the service.

Advertising

The App does not show ads and does not use your data for advertising.

Notifications

Reminders (such as daily expense reminders, due payments and budget alerts) are scheduled on your device. They are not sent from a server.

Your choices and consent

You decide whether to turn on Auto-capture, notification reminders and backup, and you can change each in Settings. You can revoke any permission in your device’s Android settings (Settings → Apps → Floosi → Permissions).

Third-party services

  • Google Play Services and Google Drive
  • Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, Performance Monitoring and Remote Config

Data sharing

I do not sell your personal information. Your financial records, messages and notifications are not shared with anyone. Data from Firebase is processed by Google as described above, and is encrypted in transit.

Data retention and deletion

I do not hold an account or profile for you. Your records are deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its storage; delete any Google Drive backup yourself as described above. Analytics and crash data are retained according to Firebase’s standard retention settings. To ask about data associated with your device, contact me at the address below.

Children’s privacy

The App is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed to children. I do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information, contact me and I will take action.

Changes to this policy

Privacy Policy

Chromix: Color Sort Puzzle · Last updated: November 15, 2026

This policy explains how the mobile game Chromix: Color Sort Puzzle (the “App”), published by Amer Mahsoub (“I”, “me”), handles information. The App is free, works offline, and has no accounts or sign-in.

Information stored on your device

Your progress, settings, scores and unlocks are stored locally on your device. I do not receive them. Uninstalling the App removes this data.

Information collected automatically

When the App is configured with Google services, the following may be collected:

  • App activity — how you interact with the App (for example, levels played), collected through Firebase Analytics to understand and improve gameplay.
  • Crash logs and diagnostics — collected through Firebase Crashlytics to find and fix crashes.
  • Advertising ID — used by Google AdMob to show and measure ads. Firebase Analytics itself does not collect the advertising ID.

The App does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise or approximate location.

Advertising

The App is supported by ads served by Google AdMob. Rewarded video ads are optional and shown only when you choose them, for hints and double XP on the results screen. Interstitial ads appear only at natural breaks, when you continue from the level-complete screen, and never during play.

Your choices and consent

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, and in certain other regions, the App shows Google’s consent form before ads or analytics are enabled. Where available, you can change your choice later under “Privacy choices” in the App’s settings. You can also reset or disable your advertising ID in your device’s Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Third-party services

  • Google Play Services / Google AdMob
  • Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics

Data sharing

I do not sell your personal information. Data is processed only by the Google services listed above, and is encrypted in transit.

Data retention and deletion

I do not hold an account or profile for you. Analytics and crash data are retained according to Firebase’s standard retention settings. To ask about data associated with you, contact me at the address below.

Children’s privacy

The App is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed to children. I do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information, contact me and I will take action.

Changes to this policy

I may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
